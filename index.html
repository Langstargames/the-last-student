THE CASE

Northview High went quiet.

Nohelle and Nathaniel were the school's golden children. They were always together—until a rainy, cloudy day when both twins disappeared. The police found no answers, and weeks later the school quietly shut down.

Years later, two friends enter the abandoned school to investigate the cold case and discover that the truth behind the closure may be much darker than the town's rumors.
